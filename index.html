Common questions

How do homeowners send their quote without a backend server?

When a homeowner clicks Request Official Booking, the widget builds a formatted quote slip containing their selected service, scope, itemized range, deposit, and optional contact notes. One tap opens their email client (mailto:) or phone messaging app (sms:) pre-addressed to the contractor, or copies the slip straight to their clipboard.

Will this work on WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, or Webflow?

Yes. Upload index.html to your hosting (or free static hosting like Cloudflare Pages or Netlify) and paste the generated <iframe> snippet into any Custom HTML or Embed block. Locking the URL with ?embed=1&trade=roofing&theme=clean strips all preview bars, and the included 7-line resize script automatically adjusts iframe height so there are never double scrollbars.

Can I change the currency, financing APR, or add a different trade?

Yes. Change currency (e.g., £, €, A$, C$) and locale at the top of TRADE_WIDGET_CONFIG, set financingApr: 0 for 0% promo plans, or duplicate any trade object to spin up landscaping, fencing, solar, or cleaning estimators in minutes.

Are the sample prices accurate for every city?

The pre-loaded numbers are realistic US residential ballpark placeholders so the widget works immediately out of the box. You should update each trade's baseMin, baseMax, and perUnit rates in TRADE_WIDGET_CONFIG to match local contractor pricing before going live.

What is the difference between the Single-Site and Agency licenses?

Both tiers give you the exact same 5-trade source bundle. Single-Site covers one business website; Agency covers unlimited client installations and custom trade builds (you just cannot resell the widget itself as a standalone template on marketplaces).
